About Harazi Global Trading

Harazi Global Trading is the international wholesale and sourcing identity operated by Harazi General Trading Sdn. Bhd., a company registered in Malaysia. We focus on connecting business buyers with suitable suppliers of food products, FMCG and selected commodities.

What We Do

We support B2B inquiries, product sourcing, supplier coordination, quotation preparation and international trading arrangements. Our focus includes Malaysian palm oil and edible oils, condensed and evaporated milk, Malaysian consumer products and international branded FMCG sourced through third parties.

Our Approach

We seek to provide clear product specifications, commercially realistic quotations and transparent shipping terms. Stock, origin, certifications, brand authorization and delivery options are confirmed on a transaction-by-transaction basis.
