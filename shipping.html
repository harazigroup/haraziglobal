International Shipping & Trade Policy

Harazi Global Trading handles business-to-business sourcing requests and explores shipment arrangements for international wholesale buyers.

Shipping destinations

We welcome inquiries from the Gulf, Africa and other international markets. Availability is conditional on supplier authorization, regulatory eligibility, logistics feasibility and the parties' written agreement.

Freight and Incoterms

Each confirmed offer should state the agreed Incoterms® 2020 rule, named port/place, who arranges freight and insurance, and which party bears customs and other charges. Incoterms alone do not settle ownership or payment conditions.

Lead times and availability

Any estimated preparation, dispatch, transit or delivery timing is provisional until confirmed in the sales contract. Customs inspections, documentation, port congestion and supplier availability can affect timing.

Import and export compliance

Required import permits, destination food standards, labeling, halal or other certification, sanctions checks and trademark rights must be verified for each shipment. Palm oil transactions are subject to applicable MPOB requirements where relevant.

Inspection and claims

Product quality, shelf life, inspection rights, required documents, shortage claims and dispute procedures must be defined in the written sales contract.

Returns and refunds

No universal return or refund policy is offered for negotiated B2B shipments. Applicable remedies are set out in the accepted contract and mandatory law.
